# Audit of the 50 verified performance controls

Audited on 29 October 2026, Asia/Kolkata. Source commit: `a87375aeb4431d81089537be8fe4418f6a505cc9`.

**Verdict: 49 rows are Verified; Slow query alert configured is Partial.** The five controls E29 had downgraded to Partial were revisited. The four cache rows are Verified in a narrow scope: every entry point now logs one `[CACHE_TELEMETRY]` line with hit, miss and eviction counts and rates, and misses are split into cold and post-eviction. The slow-query row stays Partial: the `[SLOW_QUERY_ALERT]` warning has no route or recipient. The current applicable checklist totals are **49 Verified, 71 Partial, 32 Open, 56 Unverified and 34 Managed (242 rows)**.

The unit being counted is a checklist row. Batching supports two rows; cache instrumentation appeared in four rows. Counts must not be interpreted as 50 independent optimizations or a readiness percentage. A scoped Verified label means its documented implementation, design decision or local measurement is directly supported. It does not certify the title as an unrestricted whole-system or production claim.
